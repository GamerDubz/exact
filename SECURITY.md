# Security policy

Do not open a public issue for a vulnerability. Email
[vulnerability-disclosures@expo.dev](mailto:vulnerability-disclosures@expo.dev), Expo's
disclosure contact (<https://expo.dev/.well-known/security.txt>), and say that the
report concerns Exact.

Include the host (web, macOS, iOS, Linux, Android, Windows or the terminal), the commit
of your checkout, how to reproduce it, and what an attacker gains. A minimal app that
demonstrates it is better than a description.

Known problems, including open security ones with their severity, are recorded in
[`issues/`](issues/). A report that adds to one of them is still welcome.

Exact has no releases yet: a fix lands on `main`, and the issue that recorded the
problem is closed with it.
