# Contributing

Exact is early and changes quickly, so for now the way to contribute is feedback, not
code.

**Report what you hit.** Open an issue; the feedback template asks for what matters. A
bug, a confusing or wrong doc, something an agent could not get Exact to do, a missing
capability, and a platform tell (a screen that someone who knows the platform would
notice is not native) are all wanted. Say what you did, what you expected, what
happened, and on which host. The most useful thing to include is the smallest
`app.contract` that shows the problem, or the prompt you gave an agent.

It is 100% fine and encouraged to have automatic feedback submitted by your agents. 
You can also write stuff by hand too, but don't feel like you have to.

**Pull requests are not taken at the moment.** Issues are worked by coding agents under
the maintainers' direction, and the code moves faster than outside changes can be
reviewed against it. If you have a fix, describe it in the issue, as a diff or in words:
it will be used.

**A vulnerability** goes to the contact in [SECURITY.md](SECURITY.md), not to an issue.
